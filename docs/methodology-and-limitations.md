# Methodology, evidence standards and limitations

## Workflow
1. Record the customer request or internal control question and assign an owner.
2. Map it to an applicable policy or control. Use SOC 2 references only as illustrative mappings.
3. Request evidence that demonstrates the control actually operated, not merely that a policy exists.
4. Check dates, population completeness, scope, approvals, exceptions and evidence integrity.
5. Separate observed facts, team-reported outcomes, preliminary risk judgments and approved decisions.
6. Escalate potential security events to Security Operations; GRC coordinates and documents, not independently asserts forensic conclusions.
7. Record remediation, testing, independent validation and final authorized closure as distinct milestones.

## Important limits
- All data are fictional training examples. No real customers, access logs, investigations, or sign-offs are represented.
- A test sample of five or ten is not sufficient to infer an enterprise-wide effectiveness percentage.
- The confirmed unauthorized login is part of the fictional scenario; the absence of identified data access in reviewed logs is not proof of no data access.
- A successful retest does not establish effectiveness over an earlier SOC 2 Type 2 examination period.
- EX-003's final authorized closure approval remains **pending**.
- Customer questionnaire content remains **draft / pending authorized disclosure approval**.
- This repository does not include a SOC 2 report or assert SOC 2 compliance.

## Evidence handling
Evidence IDs are illustrative references. Do not upload real authentication logs, private customer questionnaires, employee records, secrets, tokens, personal identifiers, or confidential policies to a public repository.
