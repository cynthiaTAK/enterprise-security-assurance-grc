# Interview walkthrough — Enterprise Security Assurance & GRC Operations

## 30-second introduction
“I built an independent, simulated GRC portfolio project covering customer security questionnaires, SOC 2 evidence requests, access reviews, exception management and remediation tracking. I focused on making decisions based on evidence rather than policy statements alone, and on keeping technical validation separate from authorized finding closure.”

## STAR example: EX-003
**Situation:** A fictional quarterly review identified a contractor account left active 12 days after termination; the scenario included post-termination login activity.  
**Task:** As the simulated GRC Analyst, assess evidence gaps, coordinate investigation and track remediation without making unsupported conclusions.  
**Action:** I documented the finding, prepared an escalation note, tracked the investigation, evaluated a failed offboarding test (4/5 passed and no failure alert), defined corrective actions, and assessed the later reported 10/10 retest against supporting evidence and sign-off requirements.  
**Result:** I produced a traceable closure recommendation while correctly leaving the finding open until final authorized approval. I also kept the customer questionnaire response pending separate approval.

## Likely interview questions
**What is the difference between control design and operating effectiveness?**  
A policy or configured process describes design; dated, scoped and approved execution evidence helps evaluate whether the control operated as intended.

**Why not close the finding after 10 successful retests?**  
A successful retest needs validated logs, scope and approvals. Even after validation, the designated closure approver must authorize closure; earlier deficiencies remain part of the audit trail.

**Does 80% mean the control is 80% effective?**  
No. It is only the observed pass rate of five simulated test cases and is not an organization-wide estimate.

**Did you work on a real SOC 2 audit or incident?**  
No. This was an independent simulated portfolio exercise built to practice audit documentation, control testing and cross-team coordination.
